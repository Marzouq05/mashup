FAQ's

How can I reset my password?
You can reset your password by clicking the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we will send you instructions to create a new password.
How can I contact customer support?
You can contact our support team through phone, email, or live chat. Our support agents are available to help you with account issues, technical problems, and general questions.
How long does it take to receive a response?
Most support requests are answered within 24 hours. Complex issues may take longer, but our team will keep you updated until the problem is resolved.
